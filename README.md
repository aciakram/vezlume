**Your ideas. In motion.**

A creative studio for organizing scripts, creating visual assets, adding narration and assembling videos for channels, brands and educators.

## Current status

**Private production beta.** The presentation is being opened to the public. Generation and public purchases remain paused while production testing and commercial setup are completed. This repository is a product showcase; the application source is private.

## The workflow

1. Start a project and bring your own script, images, clips and narration.
2. Prepare original images or animate an image with a selected AI model.
3. Arrange scenes, adjust duration, add captions and choose a narration track.
4. Review and export in your browser.

The website and studio offer English and Spanish interfaces. Narration language is a separate choice. Integrations are prepared for English and Spanish voices and short passages in further languages; production voice tests are still pending. Automatic translation, automatic dubbing and social scheduling are not included.

## Practical limits

- Vertical 9:16 or landscape 16:9 projects.
- Up to 40 scenes and 10 minutes per project.
- Browser export to MP4 where supported, otherwise WebM; keep the browser open.
- Up to 25 MB per uploaded file and 200 library files per account in this beta.
- AI video generation uses a reference image; the integration is not a complete long-video generator.
- Bring content you have the right to use. No guarantee of exclusivity, views or monetization.
